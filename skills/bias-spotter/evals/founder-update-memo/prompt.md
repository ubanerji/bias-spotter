Subject: Atlas rewrite - recommendation to continue

Team, I'm recommending we continue the Atlas platform rewrite through Q2. We have already put 14 months and roughly $2.3M into it, and walking away now would mean all of that was for nothing. I know the original estimate was 6 months, but the delays were caused by the vendor API changes and two unexpected departures, which were outside our control. The parts that have gone well, like the new billing module, are a credit to how strong the engineering team's execution has been.

I am confident we will ship by end of June. The remaining work is mostly integration, which should only take about eight weeks. I spoke with three customers last week and all three said they are excited about the new platform, so demand is clearly there. Stripe and Shopify both did major rewrites and came out far stronger, which shows this is the path great companies take. Sanjay raised concerns about the migration risk, but he has been negative about Atlas from the start, so I don't put much weight on that. Honestly it was obvious from day one that the old system couldn't scale, and everyone on the leadership team agrees we have to see this through.
