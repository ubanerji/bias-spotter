Is this biased? A friend wrote it and asked me for feedback.

We are deciding whether to move our 12-person team from a leased office to fully remote. I lean toward remote, but I want to lay out both sides. In favor: the lease costs $9,400 a month, and our internal survey (11 of 12 responded) showed 8 people prefer remote, 2 prefer the office and 1 has no preference. Against: the two who prefer the office are our newest hires, and published research on remote onboarding is mixed, with several studies finding slower ramp-up for junior staff. Our own data is too small to settle it. I also notice I personally dislike my commute, which probably colors my view, so I asked Dana, who prefers the office, to review this note and add anything I missed. She pointed out that our last two product launches were planned in person and we have no evidence yet that we can do that remotely. My proposal is a three-month trial with a monthly in-person week, and we agree now on what would make us reverse it: if onboarding time for the next hire exceeds eight weeks, or if more than three people ask to come back.
